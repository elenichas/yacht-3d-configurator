# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The primary audience is non-expert clients, collaborators, and design-minded visitors exploring what a contemporary luxury yacht could become. They should be able to form an expressive concept without understanding naval architecture, CAD, or shipbuilding terminology. More technically confident users should be able to reveal deeper controls and push the design further without turning the default experience into professional engineering software.

The product is used as a demonstration of capability, a presentation tool, and an ideation environment. It supports conversations and creative direction; it does not promise that a generated concept is construction-ready.

## Product Purpose

Create a real-time 3D yacht configurator that makes sophisticated yacht ideation approachable while preserving meaningful design depth. Users should be able to shape the yacht's character, spaces, materials, atmosphere, and selected architectural features; compare directions; present the result cinematically; and save or share a coherent concept.

Success means a first-time user can create something distinctive without instruction, while an advanced user can discover enough control to feel that the system is a genuine design instrument rather than a superficial option picker. Every output must clearly distinguish conceptual design from naval engineering and build documentation.

## Brand Personality

Architectural, cinematic, quietly confident.

The experience should evoke the calm precision of a leading architecture studio and the emotional presence of a private yacht presentation. Its voice is lucid, restrained, and informed. It guides without patronising and explains technical constraints without overwhelming the user.

## Anti-references

- Not a game-like vehicle customiser built around rewards, spectacle, or novelty effects.
- Not a yacht dealership catalogue dominated by navy-and-gold luxury clichés.
- Not a simplified colour-and-upholstery picker with little influence on the vessel's character.
- Not professional CAD or naval-architecture software exposed directly to a general audience.
- Not a clone of Foster + Partners or any named studio; architectural work may inform the standard of restraint and systems thinking, but the product needs its own identity.
- Not a tool that implies concepts are automatically engineered, compliant, priced, or ready to build.

## Design Principles

1. **Guide first, reveal depth progressively.** Make the first useful decision obvious, then expose advanced parameters in context as users seek more control.
2. **Show consequences in the vessel.** Every meaningful choice should produce an immediate, legible change in the 3D model, spatial story, or concept summary.
3. **Curate freedom.** Provide broad creative range through compatible systems and meaningful constraints, avoiding both arbitrary combinations and a narrow set of presets.
4. **Separate imagination from engineering claims.** Communicate feasibility bands, dependencies, and conceptual limitations without interrupting exploration or implying build readiness.
5. **Make presentation part of creation.** Camera, lighting, comparison, annotation, and storytelling are core tools, not an export afterthought.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for the full interface. All essential configuration actions must be operable by keyboard and understandable without relying on colour alone. Provide visible focus states, accessible names and values for 3D-linked controls, sufficient contrast, scalable text, and alternatives to precision dragging. Respect reduced-motion preferences by replacing cinematic camera movement and transitions with immediate or crossfaded state changes. The default language should favour clear everyday terms, with optional technical detail and definitions available when needed.
